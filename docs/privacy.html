Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 6, 2026

Project Jarvis ("we", "our", or "the application") is an open-source personal AI assistant application designed to operate locally on the user's personal computer.

1. Information We Collect and Access

When you choose to connect your Google account to Project Jarvis, the application accesses your Gmail data strictly through official Google OAuth 2.0 APIs. This access is used solely to provide assistant capabilities requested by you, including:

  • Reading unread email counts, subject lines, senders, and message snippets to generate voice or HUD briefings.
  • Searching your inbox for specific emails upon your command.
  • Drafting and sending email messages explicitly confirmed by you.

2. Data Storage and Retention

Zero External Transmission: Project Jarvis is an entirely localized desktop application. Your OAuth tokens, email messages, contact data, and assistant transcripts are stored strictly on your local machine (e.g., in your local application directory). We do not operate any external database or analytics server, and we never transmit, share, or sell your data to any third party.

3. Google API Limited Use Disclosure

Project Jarvis's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

4. Revoking Access

You may revoke Project Jarvis's access to your Google account at any time by deleting the local token.json file on your computer, or by visiting your Google Account Security Settings at Google Account Permissions.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions regarding this Privacy Policy, you may contact the maintainer via the GitHub Repository Issue Tracker.
