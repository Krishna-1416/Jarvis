Privacy Policy

Last revised: September 6, 2026 • Project Jarvis

Project Jarvis ("we", "our", or "the application") is a localized, privacy-first personal AI assistant application for Windows designed to run directly on your own personal device.

🔒 Core Privacy Guarantee: Project Jarvis does not operate remote servers, tracking services, or cloud analytics databases. All speech recognition, LLM reasoning, audio processing, and Gmail tokens remain 100% on your personal computer.

1. Information We Access via Google OAuth

When you choose to authenticate your Google Account with Project Jarvis, the application accesses Gmail data strictly through official Google OAuth 2.0 APIs. This access is utilized solely to power the following features on your command:

  • Reading unread email counts, senders, dates, and snippets to generate executive morning/startup briefings.
  • Searching your inbox when requested (e.g., "Jarvis, find emails from GitHub").
  • Displaying full email bodies when explicitly asked to read a message.
  • Drafting and sending email messages upon your verbal or written confirmation.

2. Data Storage and Retention

Your OAuth tokens (token.json) and credentials are stored strictly in your local application directory (data/credentials/). No emails, contact details, or voice transcripts are ever shared with or transmitted to any third-party server.

3. Google API Limited Use Requirements

Project Jarvis's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs adheres strictly to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

4. User Control & Revoking Access

You maintain complete control over your credentials at all times. You can disconnect your Google account immediately by deleting the local data/credentials/token.json file, or by revoking access at Google Account Permissions.
